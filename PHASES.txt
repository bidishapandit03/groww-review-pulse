DONE — PHASE 6: Top 3 themes + candidate quotes (PRD Step 6)
========================================================================
Commit: 91874bb "feat(aggregate): rank top themes + shortlist quotes"

Ranking formula (computed in code, weights tunable):
  Impact = 0.5 * volume + 0.3 * negativity + 0.2 * momentum
    volume     = count / max(count over themes)   -> "how many users"
    negativity = share of 1-3 star reviews        -> "how angry"
    momentum   = clamped positive WoW growth (7d vs prior 7d) -> "how urgent"
  Ties break by count. "other" never competes or surfaces.

Quote policy (decision): include clear praise AND Hinglish if understandable.
  Complaints rank first in the pool; praise stays eligible. Word bound 4-25.

Built:
- lib/pipeline/aggregate.ts — computeThemeStats, rankThemes, shortlistQuotes,
  aggregate(), wowDelta() (all pure; no LLM).
- lib/dates.ts — daysAgoIso(). lib/constants.ts — QUOTE_MIN_WORDS=4,
  QUOTE_MAX_WORDS=25, QUOTE_CANDIDATES_PER_THEME=6.
- tests/aggregate.test.ts — 9 math tests (wow, rank, momentum, quote filter).
- scripts/smoke-aggregate.ts — npm run aggregate:smoke (runs on tagged gold set;
  verified real verbatim Hinglish quotes). NOTE: numbers are gold-set-only,
  not the real window.
